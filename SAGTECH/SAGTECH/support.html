Standard incident flow

How SAGTECH triages and closes infrastructure work.

  1. 1

    Triage & severity assignment

    Critical and High tickets are acknowledged first; severity may be re-graded after diagnosis.

  2. 2

    Diagnosis & root cause

    Logs, query plans, packet captures and configuration diffs are gathered and recorded.

  1. 3

    Remediation & verification

    Fix applied in a controlled window, then verified against the original symptom.

  2. 4

    Resolution notes & closure

    Root cause and preventive action written to the ticket before it is marked Resolved.
